01 · About
I look at a mess of raw data and see a story waiting to be told. To me, there’s nothing more exciting than digging beneath the surface of numbers to uncover the hidden patterns that drive the world.

I'm a Computer Science & Business student at Lehigh University, currently building an equation-discovery module for Lehigh's STEM Visualization platform, grading MATH205 for ~300 students, and supporting international students through Lehigh's OISS.

During the time at VNU - Highschool for the Gifted, I led a volunteer IT curriculum for orphaned students in Ho Chi Minh City — which is where I first learned that good teaching and good software are the same skill: making something complicated feel obvious.
