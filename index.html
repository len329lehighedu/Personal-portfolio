About

I'm pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science & Business with a minor in Applied Mathematics at Lehigh University. My work sits at the intersection of data engineering, statistical modeling, and machine learning.

In Lehigh's Computational Simulation & Network Science Lab, I study whether reactive oral cholera vaccine request-months are associated with countries' usual level of recorded outbreak activity or with increases within a country over time. I also develop interactive scientific tools through the STEM Visualization Impact Fellowship and teach Linear Methods as a Math Department teaching assistant.

01 — Manuscript in preparation

Recorded Outbreak Activity and Reactive OCV Requests

We examined whether reactive request-months were associated with countries' usual level of recorded outbreak activity or with increases within a country over time across 25 African countries. The analysis estimates associations; it does not link individual outbreaks to requests or measure outbreak-to-request delay.
